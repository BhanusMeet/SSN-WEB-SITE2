What Is Tri Creatine?

Educational

Tri Creatine is a creatine-based supplement designed to support strength and performance during high-intensity exercise. Creatine is one of the most extensively researched sports nutrition ingredients available, with a well-established body of evidence supporting its use.

Creatine works by helping to regenerate ATP (adenosine triphosphate) — the primary energy currency used during short bursts of intense activity such as weightlifting, sprinting, and other high-power exercises. By supplementing with creatine, users can support their body's ability to perform at higher intensities for slightly longer durations.

The "Tri" in Tri Creatine refers to the combination of multiple forms of creatine in a single formula, potentially offering complementary absorption and utilisation benefits.
