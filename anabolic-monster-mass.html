What Is Anabolic Monster Mass?

Educational

Anabolic Monster Mass is a mass gainer — a high-calorie nutritional supplement designed for individuals who need to consume more calories than they burn in order to gain weight and build muscle mass. This is especially relevant during periods of intense resistance training where caloric demands are significantly elevated.

Mass gainers typically combine protein with carbohydrates and sometimes fats to provide a calorie-dense serving that would be difficult to achieve through whole foods alone. They serve as a convenient way to supplement your regular diet when your training goals require a caloric surplus.
