Verify Your SSN

Every SSN Elite product includes authentication features to ensure you receive a genuine product with the exact formulation as intended.

How It Works

Three Simple Steps

01

Locate the Code

Find the scratch authentication area on your SSN Elite product packaging. Carefully scratch to reveal the unique authentication code printed beneath.

02

Scan or Enter

You can either scan the QR code on the packaging using your phone camera, or manually enter the authentication code revealed by scratching.

03

Confirm Authenticity

The verification system will confirm whether your product is genuine and authentic, giving you confidence in what you're consuming.

Enter Authentication Code

Scratch the authentication area on your product packaging to reveal your unique code, then enter it below.

Note: This is a placeholder verification interface. When connected to SSN Elite's verification backend, this system will validate your product's authentication code in real-time. For now, use the QR code on your product packaging for immediate verification.
Alternative Method

QR Code Verification

Each SSN Elite product also includes a QR code on the packaging. Simply scan this QR code with your smartphone camera to be directed to the verification page with your product's details pre-filled.

The QR code provides the fastest and most convenient way to verify your product's authenticity directly from the physical packaging.
