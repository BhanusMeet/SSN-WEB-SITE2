What Is Performance Whey?

Educational

Performance Whey is a whey protein-based nutritional supplement designed to help you meet your daily protein requirements. Protein is an essential macronutrient that supports muscle repair, recovery, and growth — especially important for people who train regularly or lead active lifestyles.

Rather than relying solely on whole food sources to meet elevated protein needs, a whey protein supplement provides a convenient, measured way to ensure consistent protein intake. Each serving is formulated to deliver a known quantity of protein in a form that can be easily mixed and consumed.

SSN Elite Performance Whey uses Whey Protein Concentrate (WPC) as its primary protein source, complemented by a BCAA + Silk Amino Acid Blend, additional EAAs, glutamine, and a digestive enzyme blend to support absorption.

The Protein Source

Whey Protein Concentrate
Educational

Whey Protein Concentrate (WPC) is derived from milk during the cheese-making process. When milk is processed, it separates into curds (used for cheese) and a liquid called whey. This liquid whey is then filtered and dried to create whey protein concentrate.

WPC retains more of the naturally occurring nutrients found in whey compared to more heavily processed forms. It typically contains a high percentage of protein along with small amounts of fat, carbohydrates, and bioactive compounds that are naturally present in whey.

As a protein source, WPC provides a complete amino acid profile — meaning it contains all nine essential amino acids that the body cannot produce on its own and must obtain through diet.
