What Is EAA + BCAA + Glutamine?

Educational

This is a comprehensive amino acid supplement that combines three important categories of amino acids in a single formula: Essential Amino Acids (EAAs), Branched-Chain Amino Acids (BCAAs), and Glutamine.

Essential Amino Acids (EAAs) are the nine amino acids that your body cannot produce on its own. They must come from your diet or supplementation. EAAs are fundamental building blocks for muscle protein synthesis.

Branched-Chain Amino Acids (BCAAs) — leucine, isoleucine, and valine — are a subset of EAAs that play a particularly important role in muscle recovery and reducing exercise-induced muscle breakdown.

Glutamine is the most abundant amino acid in the body and supports muscle recovery and immune function, especially during periods of intense training when the body's demand for glutamine increases.
